# Operating-review pack

A lightweight set of five reports for a monthly capacity conversation. It is written for the hands-on implementation professional or lead who brings workload facts to that conversation, not for an executive persona. Every report is generated by `tools/capacity_calc.py` from the same records as the capacity snapshots; nothing in it is typed by hand, and no AI narrative is authoritative.

The bundled reports are built from synthetic data and are an illustrative example of the format. They report no historical performance.

| Report | Question it answers | Built from |
|---|---|---|
| [`capacity-summary.md`](capacity-summary.md) | Where is demand greater than supply, by role and month? | Capacity snapshots (`CAP`) |
| [`demand-vs-supply.md`](demand-vs-supply.md) | Where does demand come from, and why is supply what it is? | Demand records, training capacity, launch support, role capacity (`RCP`) |
| [`staffing-signals.md`](staffing-signals.md) | Which staffing triggers fired or cleared, which roles are on watch, and what is proposed? | Snapshot trigger states, staffing recommendations (`STR`) |
| [`training-support.md`](training-support.md) | How much capacity do training delivery and launch support consume? | Training capacity (`TRC`), launch-support demand (`GLS`) |
| [`operating-review.md`](operating-review.md) | What should the monthly conversation cover, and who decides? | The four reports above |

## Specification

Each report:

- states its purpose in one sentence at the top;
- shows only deterministic values and counts from the calculation, with IDs a reader can trace;
- marks zero-capacity months as `undefined_no_capacity`, never as 0;
- names no person as a decision target and records no decision;
- ends with the synthetic data label when built from synthetic records.

To produce the pack for a real team, replace the inputs (the R3 export, `data/synthetic/universe.yaml` or its equivalent, and the configuration), run `python tools/capacity_calc.py`, and read the regenerated files. Real team data stays private and is never committed to a public copy of this repository.

## Using it in the conversation

1. Open `operating-review.md` and walk the five questions.
2. For any role-month with a gap, open `demand-vs-supply.md` to see whether the gap comes from project work, training, launch support, leave, ramp, or mentoring.
3. For each proposed recommendation in `staffing-signals.md`, agree who decides and by when. Record the decision outside R2.
4. Treat every number as a forecast that the review can challenge. The calculation is fixed; the inputs are judgments.
